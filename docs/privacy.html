Dota Translator

Privacy

Short, because we keep nothing about players. Dota Translator has no sign-up and no server of ours: the app talks to Google with your own key, and that is all.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Who is responsible

Dota Translator is made by its developer, an individual in the European Union. For anything on this page, write to support@dotatranslator.live.

What we keep about you

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no e-mail address, no PC ID and no payment. The app does not send us your chat, your settings, your key or any usage counts, because it does not talk to us at all.

Your chat

  • Only the lines that need translating leave your PC. Lines already in English never do.
  • They go straight from your PC to Google's Gemini, under your own Gemini key, and the English comes straight back. Google handles them under its own terms for the Gemini API. On the free tier those terms let Google use what is sent to improve its products; a key on a project with billing set up is under Google's paid terms, which do not. That choice, and that relationship, are yours and Google's.
  • Nothing else goes with them: no name, no match, no Steam ID.

What stays on your PC

  • Your settings (config.json).
  • Your Gemini key, encrypted by Windows for your user. It is sent to Google and nobody else.
  • said.json: what you sent with Ctrl+Enter and how it was translated, so the same words are said the same way next time. You can read, correct or delete it.

What the app does on your PC

It reads the chat from the feed Valve built into the game (Game State Integration), compares two small spots of the screen with hero portraits to show who spoke, and presses keys when you press Ctrl+Enter. None of that is saved or sent anywhere. Besides Google's Gemini it talks only to GitHub (updates) and, for a hero portrait it cannot find in your own Dota, to Valve's image server. The app has no analytics and no tracking.

This website

The website (not the app) uses Google Analytics to count visits and clicks - which pages are read, which buttons pressed, roughly which country a visit is from. It sets cookies for that. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, which see your visit as any web host does.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about players to give, correct or delete. If you write to us by e-mail, your message is kept only as long as the conversation needs. You can also complain to your country's data protection authority.

We do not sell data, do not show ads, and never see what you say in chat.
